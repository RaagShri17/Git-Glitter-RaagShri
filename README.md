# 📚 Studiora

> **The AI Study Agent That Actually Thinks With You**

Studiora is an AI-powered adaptive study assistant designed to help students learn smarter—not just harder. Unlike traditional study planners, Studiora acts as a cognitive coach by understanding your learning progress, identifying weak areas, tracking memory retention, and creating personalized study plans.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🧠 AI Daily Check-in
- Adjusts your study plan based on:
  - Energy level
  - Available study hours
  - Subject difficulty
  - Focus level

### 📅 Smart Syllabus Planner
- Upload your syllabus PDF
- Automatically generates a personalized study schedule
- Dynamically updates as your progress changes

### 🎯 Weak Zone Radar
- Detects concepts you struggle with
- Identifies mistake patterns instead of only showing scores

### 📈 Forgetting Curve Tracking
- Predicts when you're likely to forget a topic
- Schedules intelligent revision sessions

### ⚡ Exam Triage Engine
- Prioritizes subjects when multiple exams overlap
- Helps avoid last-minute panic studying

### 🤖 Flora AI Assistant
- Academic question answering
- Study guidance
- Motivation and mental wellness support

### 📝 Mock Test Generator
- Generates mock tests from your own notes
- Ideal for revision before exams

### 👥 Peer Benchmarking
- Anonymous comparison with learners
- Track your study pace and consistency

---

## 💡 Problem Statement

Students often:

- Follow rigid study schedules
- Forget previously learned concepts
- Don't know their actual weak areas
- Struggle with multiple deadlines
- Lack personalized guidance
- Experience academic stress without support

Most existing study apps only manage tasks—they don't improve learning.

Studiora bridges this gap using AI.
